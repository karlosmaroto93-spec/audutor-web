Split groups, spotted

Platforms cap most bookings at 6 people, so a party of 14 arrives as three separate bookings and nobody notices it’s one group. Audutor matches them up — phone, email, surname, time — and applies your policy: a note on their confirmation and a heads-up to the guide.

A normal day with Audutor

No spreadsheet, no copying bookings across, no “does anyone know where the guide is?”.

8:41 AM
3 bookings land from GuruWalk, 1 from Viator. On their own. Two share a surname, 4 minutes apart: Audutor puts them together — 9 people — and flags the group policy in their confirmation.
9:15 AM
Tomorrow’s reminders go out by SMS. One replies “we’re 5 now” — the booking updates itself and the guide sees it on the manifest. (SMS, not WhatsApp: that’s what your customers actually read here.)
10:58 AM
“I’m lost, I can’t find the group.” GuIA checks their code, sends the map with the location the guide shared at the last stop, and leaves a note on the tour. Nobody had to phone anybody.
2:15 PM
End of the free tour: the guide takes the tip. Card in hand, tapped on the guide’s phone — no set amount suggested, as the CAP/ASA rules require. The review comes in the same motion. Two no-shows show up on the board: tomorrow those reminders go out earlier.
11:00 PM
The day settles itself. Tips and ticket sales, channel fees, the guide’s share and the company’s — tour by tour, ready for your accountant. Plus tomorrow’s brief: “2 things to check”.
