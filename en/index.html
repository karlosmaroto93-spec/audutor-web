A normal day with Audutor

No spreadsheet, no copying bookings across, no “does anyone know where the guide is?”.

8:41 AM
4 bookings land from two different platforms. On their own. Two share a surname, 4 minutes apart: Audutor puts them together — 9 people — and flags the group policy in their confirmation.
9:15 AM
Tomorrow’s reminders go out by SMS. One replies “we’re 5 now” — the booking updates itself and the guide sees it on the manifest. (SMS rather than WhatsApp — it reaches their phone without an app or a data plan.)
10:58 AM
“I’m lost, I can’t find the group.” GuIA checks their code, sends the map with the location the guide shared at the last stop, and leaves a note on the tour. Nobody had to phone anybody.
2:15 PM
End of the free tour: the guide takes the tip. A tap on the link GuIA sends, or the group QR — no amount is ever suggested for them. The review comes with the same tap. Two no-shows land on the board: tomorrow those reminders go out earlier.
11:00 PM
The day settles itself. Tips and ticket sales, channel fees, the guide’s share and the company’s — tour by tour, ready for your accountant. Plus tomorrow’s brief: “2 things to check”.

Talk to us about Premium

The price grows with travellers, never with the size of your team: per-user software charges you more precisely when you hire. The bigger you get, the less each traveller costs, and the formula is published right above. No small print.

Prices exclude VAT. With a Spanish NIF we invoice at 21 % and you reclaim it; elsewhere in the EU with a VAT number the invoice goes out under the reverse charge; outside the EU, no VAT.

Reminders are billed by Meta to your own account, whoever sends them, and we add nothing on top. What we do is send them only where there is real risk of a no-show, instead of messaging everyone — which is where the bill goes.
And GuIA has no per-message cost: it answers from rules, not per conversation.
We bill in euros, in the UK as everywhere else. Your bank converts at its own rate and we add no currency fee on top.

💳 Digital tips — however you take them, reconciled for you

However your team takes tips, Audutor logs and settles them. Cash or your own card reader — carry on exactly as you are, we charge nothing on those payments, and every tip is still recorded, split between guides and reconciled to the penny. That is the part nobody else does: they all aggregate the free-tour channel, none of them account for the free tour's actual income.

There is only ever a fee when Audutor takes the money: the link the assistant sends to anyone who left before they had a chance to tip, and the QR code for groups. The tip always goes straight into the guide's own account, with the review in the same motion.
And we don't charge for moving money that was already in the guide's hand — they have a card reader for that, and a cheaper one. We charge for the tip that would never have existed.
