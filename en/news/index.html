What’s new

What changes in Audutor, with its date. Newest first. Only what is already live.

7 Oct 2026
  • The tour closes itself. Half an hour after it ends, if attendance was taken, anyone who did not turn up is recorded as a no-show. Guides keep “Vinieron todos” (everyone came) to mark the rest in one go. If nobody came, confirm it explicitly in the manifest; an unmarked list is not treated as proof of zero attendance.
  • No-shows to report to each platform, ready to go. “Operaciones” (operations) shows full and partial absences, known deadlines and links to the GuruWalk, Freetour.com or Civitatis dashboard. Mark them there and tap “Ya lo he comunicado” (I have reported it). Later attendance corrections prompt you to check what you reported as well.
  • The manager's manifest, comfortable on a phone. Names are shown in full and buttons are finger-sized.
6 Oct 2026
  • More travellers in every plan, same price. Pro now includes 2,500 travellers a month, up from 1,000, and Premium 10,000, up from 4,000. They stay at €45 and €149. Above that, +€5 per 250 on Pro and +€15 per 1,000 on Premium.
5 Oct 2026
  • Change a departure or schedule time, keeping its bookings. In the manifest, “🕒 Cambiar la hora” (change the time) moves a departure earlier or later. In “Tours y salidas” (tours and departures), “✎” changes one fixed time to another. The guide sees it in their notes. On the platforms, change it there too.
28 Sep 2026
  • Move a booking to another departure. In the manifest, every booking has “Mover” (move) on its details line: pick the day and time, with free places in view. Each departure’s guide sees it in their notes. On the platform it stays where it was: change it there too.
  • Finished departures step aside. An hour after they end, they move to “Finished today”, folded at the bottom of the manifest. They are still there for closing and corrections.
  • A change to a booking Audutor didn’t have can be added from “Emails to review”, with today’s details. Before, it just sat there.
25 Sep 2026
  • A cancellation that arrives twice no longer shows up in “Emails to review”. If the platform sends another copy of the notice for a booking that was already cancelled, it is recognised as a repeat and changes nothing.
24 Sep 2026
  • The home page shows how it works. A departure sheet tells a day in three scenes: a GuruWalk email arrives and is read, the guide checks someone in, and the split balances. It has a pause button and stays still if your device asks for less motion.
  • The same lettering everywhere. The website, the demo and the app use their own fonts, served by Audutor with no third parties. Menu icons are drawings, not emoji that change from phone to phone.
  • Occupancy in the week. In “Team and availability” each departure shows its booked places against capacity (12/20), with a bar that turns gold near full and red when it goes over.
  • The guide app keeps itself up to date every minute while it is open. It doesn’t repaint when nothing changed, nor while a card is open or something is waiting to be sent.
  • Creating your account, clearer. With no connection, a notice says nothing has been created yet and what to do. And moving between steps while creating your tours, the keyboard stays where you were.
23 Sep 2026
  • GuruWalk changes reach the manifest again. GuruWalk changed the subject line of those emails and they were stopping in “Emails to review”. And an email that stopped there over something we can now read has a new “Read again” button.
  • “Your website” now comes with every plan. Your booking page, built from what you wrote in the Brain, is no longer a €9 add-on: on Essential it carries a small “with Audutor” badge, on Pro and Premium your own brand. Nobody sees it until you publish it.
  • The week, in columns. “Team and availability” shows the seven days side by side, each departure with its guide and the ones without a guide in red. On a phone, one day under the other.
  • Message everyone on a departure from its card in the manifest: by email to those who left one, with a ready-made WhatsApp for the rest. The guide sees it in the departure’s notes.
  • “How many came?” when closing the tour: all, some or none — the way GuruWalk asks it. “All” marks everyone who was still missing in one go.
  • “Suggest who” looks at the language: it says which guide speaks the tour’s language and, with the same workload, proposes them first.
  • Logging in is more direct. Creating a company becomes a link under the login, and after asking for the code a button opens your Gmail or Outlook searching for the email, spam included.
  • A cleaner panel: fields sized to what goes in them, each departure’s capacity as a bar, and alerts that no longer repeat inside the section they point to.
  • Today’s manifest, in sequence: all of today’s departures one after another, with the guide assignable from each card’s header.
  • Departures with no guide and no bookings are grouped in a short list with their dropdown, instead of one card each.
22 Sep 2026
  • Walk-ins from the office, and the manifest of any day from a calendar.
  • Recover older emails from the connected inbox, for bookings that arrived before you connected it.
21 Sep 2026
  • GuIA’s Brain is completed tour by tour and proposes related conditions — luggage, wifi, no-shows — with the sentence from your website they come from. You accept or correct.
20 Sep 2026
  • A tour in two languages no longer gets mixed up: bookings are matched by platform, name and language, and when in doubt you are asked once.
  • Connecting the inbox brings in the bookings you already had, keeping imported ones apart from those that arrive on their own.
  • Help before logging in: what to do if the code doesn’t arrive or you can no longer open your inbox.
19 Sep 2026
  • What reaches you from each platform, separately: new bookings, changes and cancellations from GuruWalk, Freetour and Civitatis.
  • Support from the panel, with its conversation, and an email alert when we reply if you turn it on.
  • If the connection drops, the panel recovers and the guide keeps seeing their day.
14 Sep 2026
  • “I can’t cover it”, without getting lost: if a guide turns down a departure with no signal, their reason still arrives, and their pending changes are sent first.
13 Sep 2026
  • Availability by time slots and, once you have reviewed the rota, a lock so it doesn’t change.
  • The week’s rota is reviewed before saving and is saved whole or not at all — never halfway.
  • A buffer between a guide’s departures, so nobody finishes one tour and starts another across town.
