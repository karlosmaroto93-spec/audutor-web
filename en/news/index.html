What’s new

What changes in Audutor, with its date. Newest first. Only what is already live.

23 Sep 2026
  • The week, in columns. “Team and availability” shows the seven days side by side, each departure with its guide and the ones without a guide in red. On a phone, one day under the other.
  • Message everyone on a departure from its card in the manifest: by email to those who left one, with a ready-made WhatsApp for the rest. The guide sees it in the departure’s notes.
  • “How many came?” when closing the tour: all, some or none — the way GuruWalk asks it. “All” marks everyone who was still missing in one go.
  • “Suggest who” looks at the language: it says which guide speaks the tour’s language and, with the same workload, proposes them first.
  • Logging in is more direct. Creating a company becomes a link under the login, and after asking for the code a button opens your Gmail or Outlook searching for the email, spam included.
  • A cleaner panel: fields sized to what goes in them, each departure’s capacity as a bar, and alerts that no longer repeat inside the section they point to.
  • Today’s manifest, in sequence: all of today’s departures one after another, with the guide assignable from each card’s header.
  • Departures with no guide and no bookings are grouped in a short list with their dropdown, instead of one card each.
22 Sep 2026
  • Walk-ins from the office, and the manifest of any day from a calendar.
  • Recover older emails from the connected inbox, for bookings that arrived before you connected it.
21 Sep 2026
  • GuIA’s Brain is completed tour by tour and proposes related conditions — luggage, wifi, no-shows — with the sentence from your website they come from. You accept or correct.
20 Sep 2026
  • A tour in two languages no longer gets mixed up: bookings are matched by platform, name and language, and when in doubt you are asked once.
  • Connecting the inbox brings in the bookings you already had, keeping imported ones apart from those that arrive on their own.
  • Help before logging in: what to do if the code doesn’t arrive or you can no longer open your inbox.
19 Sep 2026
  • What reaches you from each platform, separately: new bookings, changes and cancellations from GuruWalk, Freetour and Civitatis.
  • Support from the panel, with its conversation, and an email alert when we reply if you turn it on.
  • If the connection drops, the panel recovers and the guide keeps seeing their day.
14 Sep 2026
  • “I can’t cover it”, without getting lost: if a guide turns down a departure with no signal, their reason still arrives, and their pending changes are sent first.
13 Sep 2026
  • Availability by time slots and, once you have reviewed the rota, a lock so it doesn’t change.
  • The week’s rota is reviewed before saving and is saved whole or not at all — never halfway.
  • A buffer between a guide’s departures, so nobody finishes one tour and starts another across town.
