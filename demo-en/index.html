Channels & sync

how bookings get into the system — without depending on any single platform

The three ways in

📬 1 · Booking inbox the key one
Every platform booking arrives as a confirmation email (GuruWalk, Viator, GetYourGuide…).
ⓘ moreAudutor reads that inbox — your own address or a bookings@yourcompany alias we give you — and creates or updates the booking on the spot, cancellations and changes included. No API, no permission from the platform, nothing to change in how you work: it works with any channel from day 1.
🌐 2 · Your own booking engine
Your Audutor booking page (“your own site”): direct bookings with no commission, a manage-booking link for the customer (cancel or change in one tap) and group detection at the point of booking.
ⓘ moreThis is the channel the system pushes to grow — abandoned checkout, combos and reviews all point here.
📄 3 · Manual import

CSV or pasted text (your history when you start, or the odd one-off channel). With the AI importer you can also build whole tours from a platform listing.

Why email and not an API? Because GuruWalk doesn’t offer one (no iCal, no export either) — but the email it sends the guide is remarkably complete: it carries the booking details with the customer’s phone number and it flags cancellations and changes too.
ⓘ moreOn that basis the inbox gives full coverage without asking anyone’s permission. Where there IS an official API (Viator, with real-time webhooks) Audutor uses it as soon as you’re an approved supplier. Setup at sign-up: auto-forward the confirmation email to the alias — 2 minutes per channel.

🛡️ Inbox safety net — for when a platform changes its template

Reading emails is convenient, but platforms change their templates without warning. So the inbox doesn’t just read: it watches itself, so you’re never left blind on a Saturday morning.

1 · Spots the failure instantly

If an email doesn’t match the template it knows, the system flags it and texts you within seconds — it doesn’t wait for people to be missing at the meeting point.

2 · Never loses the booking

The email it can’t read lands in a review tray with whatever it did manage to read: you confirm it in one tap, or forward the plain text. A booking is never lost to a change of format.

3 · The template is re-learned

When a platform changes its format, it’s updated once, for every company on the system: your operation doesn’t depend on you doing anything.

Platform reconciliation

Included in the Pro plan · €79/month

Reconciliation is Pro’s return on investment in black and white: this month it clawed back £70 in no-show fees — nearly the whole subscription, from this one module.

Platforms charge per person booked, not per person who turns up.
ⓘ moreIf fewer come, the only way to get that cost back is reporting the no-show in time; once the window closes, the company absorbs it. That is why the guide adjusts the head count on the manifest (timestamped) and why you set each platform’s rule here — configurable, because every one of them is different.

From the guide’s head count plus these settings, Audutor builds the list of no-shows to report inside each platform’s deadline on its own, and works out what the company would absorb if they aren’t reported (you see it under 📈 Analytics).
